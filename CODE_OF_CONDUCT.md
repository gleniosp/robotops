# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project
spaces. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep feedback
about the work, explain disagreements, and respect privacy and requests to stop
unwanted contact.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, doxxing, and retaliation
against someone reporting a concern are unacceptable.

The [maintainer](https://github.com/gleniosp) handles conduct concerns and may
moderate content or restrict participation. Raise concerns publicly only when
safe, without private evidence or personal details. For sensitive concerns,
request a private contact route before sharing details. GitHub's reporting and
blocking tools are also available. There is no separate confidential conduct
contact or independent appeal process.

The maintainer considers the impact of the behavior and explains moderation
where practical without exposing private information. Participants can request
reconsideration or provide more information. Security reports follow
[the security policy](SECURITY.md).
